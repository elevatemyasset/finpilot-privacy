Privacy Policy

Your financial security is built into our code. Transparent data practices for a smarter financial future.

FinPilot Last Updated: April 13, 2026

FinPilot is a personal finance management tool developed by Elevate AI. This policy outlines how we protect your information while you focus on growing your wealth.

1. Our Data Philosophy

We believe your financial data belongs to you. FinPilot is designed to process information locally whenever possible, reducing the footprint of your sensitive data on the web.

2. Information We Collect

  • On-Device Data: Records of expenses, income, and budgets are stored locally using Hive.
  • Cloud Sync: If you enable synchronization, data is encrypted and managed via Firebase.
  • Portfolio link: Technical support and development attribution is managed by the developer.

3. Security Standards

Your data is protected using industry-standard encryption protocols. We utilize Firebase’s robust security rules to ensure that only you can access your synchronized financial records.

4. Third Party Services

To provide a stable experience, we use Google Play Services and Firebase Crashlytics for performance monitoring and bug reporting.

5. Contact & Support

For any privacy-related inquiries, please reach out to the developer directly via the portfolio link provided below.

6. Data Deletion & User Rights

Users of FinPilot can request deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

Data that will be deleted includes:

  • All transaction records (income & expenses)
  • Budget, category, debt data
  • Cloud sync and backup data (Firebase)

How to request deletion:

  • Use the in-app “Delete Account” option (Settings → Delete My Data)
  • Or visit: Account Deletion Request Page

Retention Policy:
Deleted data is removed from active systems immediately. Some backup logs may remain for up to 30 days for security and recovery purposes before permanent deletion.
