Account Deletion

Application: FinPilot by Elevate AI

How to delete your account

  1. Open the FinPilot app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings panel.
  3. Select the Delete My Data option.
  4. Confirm your choice in the security pop-up.

What will be permanently deleted

  • All recorded transaction histories (income & expenses).
  • Custom budgets, financial goals, debts, and custom categories.
  • All associated secure cloud backup profiles.

Processing time

Your deletion request is processed instantly. Complete purging across all distributed database nodes and historical backups finishes automatically within 30 days.
